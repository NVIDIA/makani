# Changelog

## Unreleased

**Breaking**
- Starting a run: a resume, when the run directory holds a checkpoint of the run, restores everything, the weights, the loss, the optimizer, the scheduler, the counters, the data pipeline and the noise generators, in the mode the run saves in (`save_checkpoint`), whatever the warm-start settings say. Before, it applied the `load_*` flags too, so a finetune config reset its counters, optimizer and schedule on every resume, and it read in `load_checkpoint` mode. A warm start from `pretrained_checkpoint_path` now takes over the weights only by default; `load_optimizer`, `load_scheduler`, `load_counters` and `load_loss` opt into the other states. The four trainers share this logic in `Driver.restore_run_state`, and the training scripts set up the run directory and detect a resume with `setup_training_run` (the ensemble script still decided by `load_checkpoint`). The scripts copy their command line options into the parameters with `arguments_to_params`, so the autoencoder script now honours `--enable_s3` and `--multistep_checkpoint`; an unknown `save_checkpoint` mode raises. A run resumes only from its own directory, so a relaunch has to pass the same `--run_num`.
- The noise of a model is driven through `model.noise`, a `NoiseController` that advances, snapshots, restores and re-seeds it; the model package exposes it as `ModelWrapper.noise`. `Rollout(batch, noise=model.noise)` replaces `Rollout(batch, noise_source=...)`, `RolloutStep.noise` is replaced by `noise_version`, and `predict`/`encode` no longer take a noise sample. The preprocessor's `noise_source`, `update_internal_state`, `get_internal_state`, `set_internal_state`, `set_rng`, `get_base_seed` and `get_internal_rng` are removed, and `NoiseState` moved to `makani.models.noise_controller`. Checkpoints and results are unchanged.
- A stochastic model refuses a `Rollout` that does not drive its own noise, and a step handed out by a rollout of another model. Noise versions come from one counter shared by all controllers.
- `NoiseController.set_rng` and `ModelWrapper.set_rng` take keyword arguments only (`seed=`, `reset=`), and the input noise is named `"input_noise"` in the controller. `noise_seed_reflect` is now `makani.models.noise.noise_base_seed`, and `run_eager` lives in `makani.models.noise`.
- `make_batch` takes the dynamic features as dicts, `inp_dynamic_features` and `tar_dynamic_features`, instead of `inp_zenith` and `tar_zenith`. `Preprocessor2D.flatten_history` and `expand_history` are now the functions `makani.utils.functions.flatten_history` and `expand_history`, which work on any grid and refuse a tensor of the wrong rank instead of passing it through.
- Networks can take a structured `ModelInput` (`takes_model_input = True`); flat networks are fed by a `FlatInputAdapter` as before. The model owns its noise (`InputNoise`), the wrapper only steers it; `multistep_checkpoint` redraws the noise of each step in its recompute. Checkpoint format 3: input-noise keys move to `input_adapter.input_noise.process.*`, older checkpoints are upgraded. Numerics change slightly, as appended channels are normalized separately and perturbing noise is added after the normalization.
- `IsotropicGaussianRandomFieldS2` and `DiffusionNoiseS2` are isotropic: the orders `m > 0` are drawn with `1/sqrt(2)` of the amplitude, as in torch-harmonics, and the diffusion spectrum is normalized including `l = 0`. The variance is now `sigma^2 / 2` at every latitude and for every `kT`; before, it dropped towards the poles and grew with `kT`. Samples for a given seed change.

**Data**
- The DALI loader takes the order, the names and the layouts of its outputs from one list, `SampleSource.outputs()`, and passes on any dynamic feature it names. New `batch_nbytes` and `without_target` helpers; the trainers and the inferencer move batches with `non_blocking=True`, and the autoencoder trainer no longer moves the targets it does not use.
- `Rollout` folds the ensemble into the dynamic features and timestamps of a target step once.

**Models**
- Networks can own noise processes, e.g. in latent space, listed through `noise_processes()`; rollouts drive them like the input noise.
- The stochastic interpolant is brought up to speed with the rest of the codebase: a `StepWrapper` driven by rollouts and the noise controller, configured by a `stochastic_interpolant` block. Breaking: earlier interpolant checkpoints do not load, and `n_future > 0` is refused.

**Fixed**
- `SpectralH1Loss` under spatial parallelism with more than one rank in `h`: the weights were not reduced to the local chunk of the rank, so building the loss raised an `AttributeError`.
- `LossHandler` with several losses and `n_future > 0`: the channel weights were tiled as `[loss 0, loss 1] * steps` and the multistep weights were repeated across all losses, while the losses are laid out as `loss 0: steps, loss 1: steps`. Each loss therefore got weights of the wrong loss and lead time, silently. With a single loss, nothing changes. `uncertainty_weighting` and `balanced_weighting` with `n_future > 0` crashed on the same mismatch, and now run.
- Learnable white noise (`IsotropicGaussianRandomFieldS2`) under spatial model parallelism: its `sigma_l` is sharded over `h` and `w` but was not annotated as shared over `matmul` only, so the gradient reduction summed the shards of other ranks (or failed on shards of different sizes) and `sync_params` overwrote them with those of rank 0.
- Noise under matmul parallelism: `noise_base_seed` seeded by the model rank, so the matmul ranks, which hold the same input, drew different noise. It now seeds by the spatial rank; without matmul parallelism the seeds are unchanged.
- `NoiseController.set_rng` (and `ModelWrapper.set_rng`) seeded every rank alike, so after it the spatial shards drew the same coefficients and the data-parallel and ensemble ranks the same noise. A process now lays the seed out over the ranks as it was built, through `rank_seed`, keeping centered pairs; serially the seeds are unchanged. Noise processes take a `centered` flag, which `build_noise` passes on.
- A resumed training run replayed the noise of the start of the run, since the noise generators were not checkpointed. The checkpoint now stores the generators of all ranks (`noise_rng_state`), and resuming restores them; a checkpoint without them, or one of another decomposition, resumes with fresh streams and a warning. New `NoiseController.get_rng_state` and `set_rng_state`.
- Checkpoints carry a format tag, `checkpoint_format`, with the mode they were written in, legacy or flexible, and a format version (now 1). A restore in the other mode is refused with a message naming the mode to use when the tensors would not match, i.e. with model parallelism on either side, and a checkpoint of a newer version is refused under `strict_restore`. Checkpoints without the tag still restore, with a warning.
- Checkpoints of matmul-split models: the layers that are split over the `matmul` group have the same parameters as their serial counterparts, so a flexible checkpoint restores across any decomposition. `MLP` keeps its layers as `fc1` and `fc2`, as `DistributedMLP` does, rather than in a sequence (`fwd.0`, `fwd.3`); `DistributedMatmul` keeps a 1-D bias in the nchw format, as `nn.Conv2d` does, rather than `(1, C, 1, 1)`; and `DistributedEncoderDecoder` counts `num_layers` as `EncoderDecoder` does, the hidden layers before the output layer. Before, the latter built one layer less than the serial network and refused the default of one hidden layer under matmul parallelism. The checkpoint format version is now 2: checkpoints of an earlier version, or without a format tag, are upgraded on restore, model state and optimizer state alike (`upgrade_checkpoint`), including by the checkpoint converter; those of matmul-split SFNOs cannot be, as their encoders and decoders had another structure.
- Gradient reduction under matmul parallelism: a layer that is not split over the `matmul` group computes the same on every matmul rank, so each rank holds its full gradient, but the reduction summed the gradients over the group, doubling them with two matmul ranks, or left them unreduced, so that the replicas could drift apart under reduced precision. The annotation `is_shared_mp` is now a dict from model-parallel group to reduction, e.g. `{"spatial": "sum", "matmul": "avg"}`: summed over ranks that split the computation, averaged over ranks that replicate it. All layers are annotated this way, which also sums the gradients of the q/k norms of `DistributedAttention` over its heads and marks `DistributedPatchEmbed` as split over `matmul`; a parameter without annotation is shared as `DEFAULT_SHARING`, and the list form of earlier versions is still read. `get_sharing` in `makani.mpu.helpers` reads an annotation for `init_gradient_reduction_hooks` and `sync_params`, and refuses a parameter shared over a group it is sharded over. The distributed layer tests reduce their gradients themselves.
- Attention: `MultiHeadAttention` replaces ViT's `Attention` and `DistributedAttention`, with the heads split over the `matmul` ranks if there are several. It has separate `q`, `k` and `v` projections, each split by heads, so that a checkpoint is the same for any decomposition (the fused `qkv` of `DistributedAttention` was split by contiguous rows, which interleaved the heads by the matmul size), a bias on the output projection in either case (`DistributedAttention` had none), and no biases the softmax removes: none for the keys without `qk_norm`, and none in the normalization of the keys with it. The attention dropout applies in training only. Breaking: ViT checkpoints of earlier versions do not load.

## 0.3.0

**Breaking**
- Python >= 3.11, torch >= 2.12.1, torch-harmonics == 0.9.4 (with the DISCO and zero-width halo fixes; 1.x is not compatible yet), PhysicsNeMo >= 1.3.0 (1.x and 2.x), DALI 2.x (`nvidia-dali-cuda120`), zarr >= 3. Dropped numba, pynvml, progressbar2, jsbeautifier and moviepy; matplotlib is a core dependency, and the `vis` extra installs imageio/imageio-ffmpeg. New `test`, `data_process`, `legacy_data_process` extras.
- Dataloaders emit dict batches (`input`, `target`, `*_dynamic_features`, `*_timestamp`; `(B, T, C, *grid)`). Timestamps are always present, and `return_timestamp` is gone.
- `Rollout` owns the multistep loop, and `StepWrapper` replaces `SingleStepWrapper`/`MultiStepWrapper`. The preprocessor is stateless between steps. `multistep` in `get_model`/`load_model_package` has no effect and warns, and `get_model` no longer accepts other extra arguments. `continue_noise` is replaced by `Rollout(noise_state=...)`.
- DALI dataloader state format 2 (timestamps are pipeline outputs, on the GPU): a dataloader state saved before is refused on resume, or skipped with `strict_restore: False`.
- Old DALI helpers (`data_loader_dali_2d`, `dali_es_helper_*`) were replaced by `dali_dataloader` + `sample_source`. Removed `img_utils`, `factorizations`, `drift_regularization`, `DistributedAFNO2Dv2`, and the fin/fout matmul split, which is now plain fork-join.
- SFNO: no MLP output bias before instance norms (dropped on load); the frequency `pos_embed` has a full `m = 0` column. Old checkpoints of the latter need conversion, and runs from before cannot resume their optimizer state.
- Bias, spectrum and zonal-spectrum files give lead times in hours, and the zonal spectrum's Nyquist bin is half its previous value. An unknown `scheduler` name raises. Pangu checkpoints trained before the shifted-window roll fix do not reproduce exactly.
- The ACC, SSR and spread of data with missing values come out lower: each sample's value is taken over its valid area, and the samples are averaged weighted by that area. They were too large by the inverse of the valid-area fraction (its square root for the spread).
- Invariants are stored in a single file; the model package renamed `land_sea_mask.nc` to `land_mask.nc` and normalizes its file names. Cosine zenith calculation uses an optimized vectorized routine.

**Data**
- Pluggable storage backends: makani HDF5, concatenated HDF5, makani zarr, ARCO/WB2 zarr, and native ICON mesh. Datasets declare and verify their grid. Shared channel mapping and metadata rework.
- Subsampling/resampling, NaN targets with masked losses and imputation, leap-year-safe concatenation, dataloader save/restore, GDS and O_DIRECT IO.
- Conservative remapping (raster and mesh to lat/lon), serial and spatially distributed.
- Converters: WB2 and NSF NCAR ERA5 merged into one tool with pluggable sources, NCAR downloader, h5_convert fixes, improved stats (Welford, power spectra).

**Models**
- Model package rework built on `Rollout`.
- Stochastic MLP with log-std spread, and a reworked noise module that is compilable and has a save/restore state.
- Water-channel constraint handling cleaned up in FCN3 and the constraints wrapper. Fixes to Pangu's shifted-window roll and `BandLimitMask`'s default band limit.

**Training**
- Multistep weighting modes and multistep checkpointing. The ensemble is folded into the batch dimension. Validation streams one lead time at a time. Trainers no longer read a setup batch, so the first DALI epoch is complete.
- Losses: MMD, kernel score, corrected and memory-efficient energy score, and relative losses. Fixes to CRPS gradients, the coherence loss, the energy-score spread at E=1, Gauss CRPS with ensemble weights, and the CRPS losses for single-member forecasts with spatial weights. Losses are AMP-safe.
- `torch.compile` and inductor support (step-level compilation), FP8/TE autocast options, weight-decay param groups, gradient-norm tracking, and an LR warmup start.
- Checkpoints carry a parameter-name checksum. Fixes to the distributed instance norm and to the parallel comm routines, which moved from PhysicsNeMo to TH/makani.

**Inference and tooling**
- Streaming rollout buffer (GDS, O_DIRECT), WB2-compliant output, and an inferencer built on `Rollout`. With `n_history > 0`, the buffer records the latest step of the window, at its time, as the initial condition, not the oldest.
- Metrics: ACC, SSR and spread of masked data are valid-area-weighted averages of the per-sample values; spectrum buffers count the Nyquist order once; climatology and mask lookup work with history and across leap years. `load_time_loop` steps by `dt * dhours` hours.
- Visualization rewrite: op registry instead of eval, rank 0 only, progress bars on videos; GIFs and rollout videos without moviepy, and a rollout plotting script.
- Benchmark tool, profiling/NVTX fixes, pre-commit and a style CI, CI on pull requests, API docs, SECURITY.md, and broad test coverage, including distributed tests with DALI, on CPU and under srun/mpirun. CUDA-only calls are guarded, so runs work on CPU. Docker images build for B300/GB300.
